Hero EXP: Points = 2 × (Amount EXP (M) × 1 000 000 ÷ 660). Only full blocks of 660 EXP count, so the leftover EXP is shown separately. Decimals work, for example 1.5 for 1 500 000 EXP.
Recruits and shards: Points = fixed points × amount.
“Load example” fills in 6 660 M EXP, 10 vouchers, 2 S+ Shards, 5 S Shards, 8 A Shards and 50 skill EXP books, which adds up to 20 311 318 points.

Ruins Battlefield

Four squads, four zones, plus the Big Hitters who roam freely and assist the other squads. Sign up each player with their name and CP under the right squad. Tap a squad on the map to jump to its list.

Share on Discord

Copies all squads and the Big Hitters as a ready-made list you can paste straight into Discord.

Backup and restore

Sign-ups are saved in this browser only. Copy a backup code now and then, for example into a private Discord message, so nothing is lost if the browser data is cleared. Paste a backup code below to restore it on any device.

Amount of CP (M) is in millions: type 45 for 45 M or 38,5 for 38.5 M. A full number such as 45 000 000 also works. Level is required and must be a whole number from 0 to 30. Each player can be signed up once. If a new name looks like one already on the list, you get a warning and can click Sign up again to add the player anyway. To move a player to another squad, remove them and add them again.
